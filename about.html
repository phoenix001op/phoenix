About Phoenix

Phoenix is a small anime discovery site — browse what's airing, search for a title, and rate and review anything you've watched. It's a personal project, built to be simple and useful rather than fancy.

Who made this

Hi, I'm Lavish Chauhan, 17 years old. I'm still learning to code and built this site as a personal project. I mainly know HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python.

How it works

Anime data (titles, images, genres, ratings, streaming links) comes from the free Kitsu API. Your ratings and reviews are saved locally in your own browser — nothing is sent to a server or shared with anyone else.
